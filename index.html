I am Zijun Song, also known as Purple Nightmare. I am a PhD student at the School of Advanced Interdisciplinary Sciences, University of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and I work with the Institute of Automation, CAS.

My research focuses on AI agents, especially agent reinforcement learning, tool learning, and post-training: when search, tools, and external execution systems help a model do better, which capabilities can stay in the parameters and transfer to new tasks? Recent work includes fine-grained credit assignment for search agents, trigger-based supervision for continual training, and long-horizon exploration for edge-scale agents.

I also work on AI for Science. I am first author of PertDiffBench, a benchmark for diffusion models in single-cell perturbation response prediction.

I received my B.Eng. from the School of Software, Dalian University of Technology. Feel free to email me about methods, reproduction, or open-source work.
